📲 Add to Home Screen Required

iOS Safari requires adding this web app to your Home Screen to support Web Push notifications:

  1. Tap the Share button (box with up arrow) in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open BuzzMe from your Home Screen to enable alerts.

🚀 Outbound Buzz (iOS Shortcut)

Send 1-tap buzzes from iOS Home Screen or Lock Screen to other household devices.

  1. On your Android Host, tap "Pair Device" → "iOS Outbound Shortcut".
  2. Scan the setup QR code shown on the Android Host screen.
  3. Verify the 6-digit code matches on both screens.
  4. Host taps Approve, then tap Confirm & Install to save your Shortcut token.
